ListyGifty is now on ChatGPT, Claude, Runner, GrokBot and more

You can now save gift ideas to ListyGifty right from a ChatGPT conversation. When you’re at the store, just ask, “Which scarf did Dad want again?”

ListyGifty also works with Claude, Runner, GrokBot, and other compatible AI tools.
Set up another assistant → https://listygifty.com/integrations#connect

A book for Alice
