These terms cover using Listy Gifty to plan gifts, keep wishlists, and run gift exchanges.

## Key Points

- You must be at least 13 to use Listy Gifty.
- Your content stays yours. Listy Gifty only uses it to run the service for you and the people you share with.
- People you share a list, wishlist link, or exchange link with can see what you shared.
- Gift exchange invites and name draws email people on your behalf, so only invite people who expect to hear from you.
- Connected assistants such as ChatGPT and Claude act on your instructions within the permissions you approve. You can disconnect them at any time.
- Paid plans show their price and billing terms before you pay.
- No spam, harassment, unlawful use, or attempts to access data that is not yours.
- Delete your account from the mobile app's Profile screen or by emailing support@listygifty.com.
