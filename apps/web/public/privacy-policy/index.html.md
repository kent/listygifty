# Listy Gifty Privacy Policy Summary

Listy Gifty helps users organize gifts, people, and occasions. The privacy policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and which controls are available.

## Information Collected

- Account information such as name and email.
- App content created by users, including gift lists, people, gifts, notes, wishlists, and occasions.
- Usage data needed to operate, secure, diagnose, and improve the service.

## Sharing and Access

Lists are private by default unless users explicitly share them. Collaborators can access only the list they were invited to. Integrations use OAuth with scoped permissions, and connected tools can only access data allowed by the granted scope.

## Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to support@listygifty.com.
