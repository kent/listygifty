# Business Gift Planning for Teams

Listy Gifty business workspaces help teams coordinate employee and company gifting without spreadsheets.

## Summary

The business gifting workflow supports recipient imports, shipping address capture, bulk gift creation, missing-data review, gift status tracking, and fulfillment-ready exports.

## Use Cases

- Remote-team holiday boxes
- New-hire onboarding kits
- Work anniversaries
- Promotions and milestones
- Parental leave gifts
- Client gifts
- Team celebration gifts

## Workflow

1. Import employees, recipients, notes, and default shipping addresses from CSV.
2. Create or import gifts and match them to recipients by name or email.
3. Track cost, purchase status, links, recipient details, and giver details.
4. Review gaps such as missing recipients, costs, links, or shipping addresses.
5. Export people and gifts with fulfillment-ready address fields.
