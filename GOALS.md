# ListyGifty — Goals

## What it is

ListyGifty is a tool for keeping the gift side of life organized — for families, friend groups, and teams. Built originally because Kent and Christina hit the wall trying to manage **50+ Christmas gifts** across friends and family. The list lives in your head, the ideas fade by November, the orders get scattered across five tabs.

ListyGifty fixes that. One place to:

- **Track who you're giving gifts to** (and who's getting what).
- **Capture ideas year-round** so December isn't a panic.
- **Run gift exchanges** — Secret Santa, Yankee Swap, "everyone draws one name" — without spreadsheets.
- **Eventually one-tap order** the gift instead of hunting it down on Amazon.

## Who it's for

- **Busy parents** managing the family gift logistics — birthdays, holidays, kids' classmates, teachers, in-laws.
- **Friend groups** running annual gift exchanges who currently use a thread + a spreadsheet + a missed deadline.
- **Companies & teams** organizing employee gifts — onboarding gifts, work anniversaries, holidays, client gifts. The biggest budget but the most painful workflow today.

## Why it has a shot

- Christmas isn't going anywhere; the use case repeats every year with high emotional stakes.
- The current tooling is genuinely bad — Notion docs, Google Sheets, screenshots in iMessage threads.
- Mobile-first matters: gift ideas happen *out in the world* (in a store, at a coffee shop, scrolling Instagram), not at a desk.
- Two markets, one product: consumer pulls users in via the holiday spike; business gifts is where the revenue lives.

## Definition of done — next 6 months

**Consumer (the wedge):**
- Lists, gift ideas, and recipients fully working in the mobile app — capture an idea in 5 seconds while standing in a shop.
- Secret Santa / gift-exchange flows shipped: create an exchange, invite people, draw names, set budget, set deadline.
- TestFlight → public App Store launch.
- 100 households actively using the app for their 2026 Christmas planning by November.

**Business (the revenue):**
- A "Teams" plan exists with company billing, multi-admin, and bulk gift workflows.
- 5 paying companies running their employee gift program through ListyGifty.
- At least one playbook ("onboarding gift kits," "remote-team holiday box") templated and reusable.

**Product:**
- One-tap purchase flow against at least one merchant (Amazon affiliate or a curated catalog) — the "ideas → ordered" loop closed for at least the simple case.
- Calendar sync: birthdays and gift-giving dates surfaced before you forget.

## Non-goals (next 6 months)

- Wedding registries. Adjacent but a different product.
- A full e-commerce storefront. Curate / link out / affiliate — don't run inventory.
- Charity gifting / donations-in-someone's-name. Defer until the core loop works.
- AI-generated gift recommendations as a flagship feature. Useful, but not the wedge — list management is.

## Why now

Kent and Christina will use it personally for Christmas 2026. That's a hard, real-world deadline that will surface every gap. Build for that, ship for everyone else.
